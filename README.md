**Follow-up assignment** to the OECD presentation:

---

## Big Idea

> Moderate inflation -- kept between 1.5% and 3% -- is not the enemy.
> It is the economy's ignition switch: it rewards borrowers, motivates spending, erodes
> the real burden of debt, and signals that businesses are growing and hiring.
> **The real danger is not inflation itself -- it is losing control of it.**

## Design Standards

- **Format:** 16:9 widescreen (13.33" x 7.5")

- **One idea per slide** -- no text walls
- **Colour system:** Green = healthy/good, Amber = caution, Red = danger, Yellow = key insight

## Key Economic Arguments

1. **The Spending Incentive** -- rising prices today motivate spending now, not later
2. **The Debt Erosion Effect** -- fixed-rate debt shrinks in real terms
3. **The Wage Adjustment Buffer** -- nominal rigidity is easier to manage with mild inflation
4. **Monetary Policy Room** -- central banks need inflation above zero to cut rates in a crisis
5. **The Goldilocks Zone** -- 1.5-3% is the global central bank consensus target
6. **OECD Evidence** -- 8 economies averaged 2.39% over 2000-2024, within the healthy range
